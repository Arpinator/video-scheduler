Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Video Scheduler is a personal automation tool used only by its developer to upload videos to the developer's own YouTube channel and the developer's own TikTok account.

Information we access

YouTube / Google

The app requests access to the developer's Google account through the YouTube Data API solely to upload and schedule videos and set their titles, descriptions and thumbnails on the developer's own channel.

TikTok

The app uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API. With the developer's authorisation, it accesses basic profile information (scope user.info.basic) and the permission to publish videos (scope video.publish) to the developer's own TikTok account. It is used solely to post videos created by the developer to that account.

How information is used and stored

  • Authorisation tokens for YouTube and TikTok are stored as encrypted secrets in the developer's private automation environment and are used only to upload the developer's own videos. They are never shared.
  • The app does not collect, store or sell personal data from any other user.
  • No data is shared with third parties.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data deletion and revoking access

  • Google / YouTube: revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • TikTok: revoke access in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services (or Manage app permissions).
