Short videos, published on schedule.

Z-app Video Scheduler is the publishing tool behind the Zoldi channel. It renders short vertical videos from a script and posts them automatically to the creator's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts, every week.

See how it works

What it does

Script to video

Each episode starts as a short script. The tool renders it into a 1 to 1.5 minute vertical video with animation, captions and voice-over.

Weekly schedule

Videos go out on fixed days at 6am UK time, so the channel stays consistent without manual uploads.

One run, every platform

A single publishing run posts the same video, title and caption to YouTube, Instagram Reels and TikTok.

Own accounts only

The tool posts only to accounts owned by its creator, using each platform's official API and the creator's own authorisation.

What gets published

Tech News

A one-minute bulletin of three stories from AI, Kubernetes, cloud and infrastructure.

Tech Tips

Practical DevOps and infrastructure tips explained in 90 seconds with charts and a cartoon mascot.

Detective Z

A mouse detective solves a short mystery each episode.

Cat stories

Funny animated stories about a real house cat and his daily antics.

Last Stop

A choose-your-path horror series where viewers pick the next turn in the comments.
