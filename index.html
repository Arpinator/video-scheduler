Video Scheduler

Video Scheduler is a personal automation tool that uploads and schedules short videos created by its owner to the owner's own YouTube channel (using the YouTube Data API) and to the owner's own TikTok account (using TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API).

It is not offered to the public and is used only by its developer. It does not collect any data from other users.
